## Areas for Improvement

### 1. Grammatical Accuracy

Focus on improving article usage (a/an/the) and subject-verb agreement. For example, instead of "the weather conditions feels cozy," say "the weather conditions feel cozy."

### 2. Natural Collocations

Practice using more natural collocations and idiomatic expressions. Instead of "bursting hot," consider "scorching hot" or "blistering heat."

### 3. Sentence Structure

Work on sentence structure variety to avoid overly long and complex sentences that sometimes lose clarity. Break down some longer explanations into shorter, more digestible sentences.

### 4. Prepositions and Phrasal Verbs

Pay attention to prepositions and phrasal verbs. For instance, "take a consider of" could be "take into consideration" or "consider."

## Vocabulary Notes

### Key Terms Used

1. **Scorching hot**: Extremely hot. (e.g., "It was scorching hot, making outdoor activities unbearable.")
2. **Invigorating**: Making one feel strong, healthy, and full of energy. (e.g., "The cool, invigorating wind made the bike ride very pleasant.")
3. **Picturesque**: Visually attractive, especially in a quaint or pretty style. (e.g., "The route offered picturesque views of the coastline.")
4. **Seamlessly**: Smoothly and continuously, without interruption. (e.g., "The public bike system allows you to seamlessly rent and return bikes across the city.")
5. **Serene**: Calm, peaceful, and untroubled; tranquil. (e.g., "Riding at night offers a serene atmosphere, away from the daytime crowds.")
6. **Unforeseen**: Not anticipated or predicted. (e.g., "I stumbled upon an unforeseen mini-golf competition during my ride.")
7. **Navigating**: Planning and directing the route or course of a ship, aircraft, or other form of transport, especially by using instruments or maps. (e.g., "The app helps in navigating routes and avoiding potential hazards.")
8. **Conducive to**: Making a certain situation or outcome likely or possible. (e.g., "The wide paths are conducive to a comfortable and safe biking experience, even with many riders.")
