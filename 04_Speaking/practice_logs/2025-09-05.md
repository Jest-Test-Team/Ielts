## Vocabulary Notes

### Key Terms Used

1. **Holistic approach**: Considering the whole of something and how all its parts are interconnected. (Example: 'Maintaining health requires a holistic approach, encompassing diet, exercise, and mental well-being.')
2. **Intrinsic motivation**: Doing something because it is inherently rewarding, rather than for external rewards. (Example: 'His dedication to biking stems from intrinsic motivation, not just competitive ranking.')
3. **Optimized**: Made the best or most effective use of a situation, resource, or system. (Example: 'The software helps me track and optimize my training sessions for peak performance.')
4. **Pragmatic**: Dealing with things sensibly and realistically in a way that is based on practical rather than theoretical considerations. (Example: 'Her pragmatic approach to work-life balance ensures she allocates time effectively.')
5. **Conducive to**: Providing the right conditions for something good or successful to happen. (Example: 'A quiet environment is conducive to focused work and deep concentration.')
6. **Discerning**: Having or showing good judgment. (Example: 'A discerning user will appreciate the nuanced features of this advanced fitness tracker.')
7. **Synergy**: The interaction or cooperation of two or more organizations, substances, or other agents to produce a combined effect greater than the sum of their separate effects. (Example: 'The synergy between the hardware and software creates a powerful tracking ecosystem.')
8. **Mitigate**: Make less severe, serious, or painful. (Example: 'Regular exercise can help mitigate the risks associated with a sedentary lifestyle.')
