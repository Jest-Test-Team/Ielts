## Vocabulary Notes

### Key Terms Used

1. **No-brainer**: Something that is very easy to decide or understand. (e.g., 'Choosing to travel by plane was a no-brainer for me.')
2. **Allocated**: Distributed for a particular purpose or to particular places. (e.g., 'The planes were allocated to their respective gates.')
3. **Enhancement version**: An improved or more advanced version of something. (e.g., 'The new model is an enhancement version of its predecessor.')
4. **Superposition**: A state of being placed or laid over something else; in a more abstract sense, a combination of multiple states. (e.g., 'The various factors influencing travel are in a state of superposition until experienced.')
5. **Propensity**: An inclination or natural tendency to behave in a particular way. (e.g., 'He has a propensity for exploring unique travel destinations.')
6. **Verifiable**: Able to be checked or demonstrated to be true, accurate, or justified. (e.g., 'Always seek verifiable sources when researching travel information.')
7. **Traceable**: Able to be found or followed by tracing. (e.g., 'Ensure the information comes from a traceable source.')
8. **Conception**: The forming or devising of a plan or idea. (e.g., 'My conception of travel has evolved over time.')
