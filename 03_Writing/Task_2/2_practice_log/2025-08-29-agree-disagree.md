# IELTS Writing Task 2 Practice

**Date:** August 29, 2025  
**Task Type:** Agree/Disagree  
**Topic:** Science and Human Life Improvement

---

## Task Instructions

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

**The most important aim of science should be to improve people's lives.**

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

---

## Student Response

It can be argued that major focus for science should be improving people's lives.

In this essay, I will demonstrate concepts as well as real-world examples to show my agreement upon this narrative and support it.

Firstly, I will explore some well-known figures to show the importance of using science for benefiting of human's lives.

And secondly, some examples to backing these ideas up.

Nobel's peace price, known as the most honorable achievement to accept globally, was ironically founded by a chemist Nobel, the inventor of dynamites.

Though his creations have been used for wars and unethical activities, Nobel's intention for creating dynamites was originally toward contributing to constructions like tunnels.

He founded foundation named after him, and specifically established peace category to remind our world that creations of science shall only be used for improving people's lives.

Issac Asicmof, the creator of three laws for robots, also mentioned how worse situations can be.

If science is used for intentions other than improving people's lives, like robots controlled by villains in his works, our beloved societies will collapse and fall.

Nuclear power, for instance, can be used to generating completely clean energy for people's daily life.

But it can also be weaponized to extinguish entire countries within seconds, as Noble predicted.

Another example, stunt-net, a form of cyber attack involving quantum computing and artificial supreme intelligence, happened in Iran

This invisible attack suspend all infrastructures from functioning, causing financial, trasportationl, even medical loss.

And that, cited from Issac's work about virtual invasions with robots, was like arrows from thousand of hands of Shiva's, which was deadly, and unavoidable.

To sum up, great figures have warned us must use science productions wisely for improving people's lives.

And several existed incidents, have prove them to be right, for a certain degree.

In my opinion, only if science is used for well purposes then the world can thrive.

Otherwise, horrible consequences will affect not just us, but for generations.
