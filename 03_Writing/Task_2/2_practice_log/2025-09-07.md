# IELTS Writing Task 2 - Discussion Essay

**Time:** 40 minutes  
**Word Count:** At least 250 words

## Question

In many countries, primary and secondary schools close for two months or more in the summer holidays.

What is the value of long school holidays?

What are the arguments in favour of shorter school holidays?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

---

## Answer

Some primary and secondary schools aren’t operating for 2 months or longer time during summer holidays globally.

In this essay I will give examples for both potential values of longer and shorter school holidays.

Firstly I’ll depict how long summer holidays  can contribute in several ways and secondly what shorter ones can achieve positively.


Students enjoying summer holidays with longer period can possibly explore more places, fields of habit, and social relationships more easily.

And with the activities related to these topics, expenditure will be a must, which can temporarily boost macro economics.

For example, students in Taiwan nowadays occupied summer holidays last around 2.5 month, and most students enjoy this vacation a lot.

They allocate their free time into visiting different cities in Taiwan with their friends, learning instrument playing or dancing, or simply watch movies with peers form classes or school clubs.

All these activities may be considered as beneficial for  students.

Since constructive effects such as precious memories, technical skills developing, and social relationships expanding are all involved, not to mention the allowances students contribute to society.


However, shorter summer holidays can release parents’ burden both in the aspects of money,  time managing as well as  security  issues.

Also, ass the marginal effect of having complete freedom faded, students may become knowing not what to do at the end of their vacation.

Using the same example above, parents in Taiwan have to be responsible for the activities’ fees, which can be expensive.

Also, parents have to track or pickup their kids in more various time and less predicable locations.

And these activities and related locations sometimes can be  dangerous, like camping fields in deep mountains area or dangerous seashores.

As for students themselves, after watching 2 months of movies and to karaokes, most Taiwanese students already got very bored and started to swipe phone all-day long.

Therefore, shorter summer holidays are not only possibly being able to reduce parents burden of economic as well as mental, physical health, but may also keep students safe and motivated. 



To sum up, longer time for summer holidays can let students develop more and may boost national level economic.

While shorter ones more likely to relive their parents and assure students in well status, especially mentally.

They both showed unique values, and it's worthy considerations to assess which to be implemented to suit various conditions.
