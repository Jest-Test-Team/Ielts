# IELTS Writing Task 2 - Opinion Essay

**Time:** 40 minutes  
**Word Count:** At least 250 words

## Question

Some cities create housing areas by providing taller buildings. Others create housing by building houses on a wider area of land. What solution is better?

---

## Answer


Housing areas in cities can be planned implemented via taller constructions or simply using larger land area.

In this essay, I'll discuss both solutions with real world examples.

Firstly, I will explore using taller constructions and secondly using larger land area.

Some locations in middle-east region, usually surrounded by dessert, are not able to spread size of cities.

Because it may be too far away to water source, and it's extremelly expensive to build water pipeline in these areas.

Tall buildings, however, perfectly solve this problem.

By developing vertical, residents can be as close as possible to water source, horizontally.

And vertical distance issues can be eassily addressed by modern solar energy motor plumps.

Thus expanding tall buildings can be a perfect solution for cities located in middle-east areas.

On

the other hand, some cities on tropical or subtropical island, like

Green Island of Taiwan, should implement larger land area housing

strategy.

Since they may facing lots of typhoons or other natural

disastrs that are specificly dangerous for tall buildings, heights of

constructions shall remain low.

Also, most of them occupied stable

as well as various sources of water, like underground water, natural

fountain, even post-processed sea water.

Hence, it would be more

proper for them to take advantage of using larger land area to build

accomodations or any facilities with low heights.

In conclusion, these 2 solutions are suitable for different cities with various natural limitations.

When cities are surrounded by dessert, constructing tall buildings could be the better one

But cities located on tropical island should consider more about building houses on a wider area of land.
