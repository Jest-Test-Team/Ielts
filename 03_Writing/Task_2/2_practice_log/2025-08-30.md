# IELTS Writing Task 2 Practice

**Date:** August 30, 2025  
**Task Type:** Advantages vs Disadvantages  
**Topic:** Aging Population

---

## Task Instructions

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

**In many countries, people are now living longer than ever before.**

Some people say an aging population creates problems for governments.

Other people think there are benefits if society has more elderly people.

To what extent do the advantages of having an aging population outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

**Minor Slips:** A few minor errors (How ever for However, assurances for insurance, young-aged labors for young laborers) are present but are infrequent and do not affect comprehension.

#### Grammatical Range and Accuracy: Band 7.0 (The Final, Stubborn Enemy)

Your sentence structures are varied and complex, and your overall control is good. However, your most persistent grammatical enemy has reappeared.

**The Problem:** The sentence fragment.

**Your sentences:**

- By which, not only elders were benefited...
- By which, can deeply infect economy Taiwan...

**The Rule:** Remember our "Train Hook Rule." A clause starting with which is a "train car" that must be attached to the main sentence "engine." It cannot start a new sentence.

**The Fix:**

- ...created large amount of job opportunities, which not only benefited elders...
- ...for they and their assets are untraceable, which can deeply infect the economy of Taiwan...

### Action Plan: Defeating the Final Boss

You have mastered every other aspect of IELTS writing. This one, single, recurring error is the final boss.

**Your Final Mission is to achieve ZERO fragments.**

During your proofreading stage, your only job is to hunt for sentences starting with Which or By which and ensure they are correctly attached to the previous sentence with a comma.

This is a simple mechanical fix. Once you eliminate this habit, your writing will be virtually flawless. Congratulations on another brilliant performance.
