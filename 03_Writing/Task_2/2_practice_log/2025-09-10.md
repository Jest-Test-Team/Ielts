# IELTS Writing Task 2 - Opinion Essay

**Time:** 40 minutes  
**Word Count:** At least 250 words

## Question

In many countries women and men are working full time. It is therefore, logical for women and men to share household works. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

---

## Answer

In this essay, I'll give examples in logical aspects to show my agreement on males and females should share household works equally.

Firstly, I'll discuss the reason why equal household workloads allocations is logical and secondly the inrational parts of unequal household workloads allocations.


It's crystal clear that if male and female are living together, it's possible for they to share same right and responsibility to enjoy their living space as well as maintain it.

So in most double-paid cases, according to general belives:every individuals are equal, household workloads allocations should be allocated equally.

For me, my parents shared chores equally for decades, and they have fewer fights than other parents of my friends or classmates.

Hence, it's not only more logical but also more practical.


On the other hand,I discovered that some male college roommates of mine, are not able to do basic chores.

Including fundamental cooking, cleaning rooms, even doing their own laundries.

Their responses to these disabilities are that their mothers did all the house works, while their fathers didn't.

And that made them to consider chores are for weman, even their parents both occupied full-time jobs.

In most cases, they do have argues with their girlfriend or female roommates about allocations regarding house works.

Since they both paid same amount of rents but females have to do most chores, which can be viewed as unfair.

These females claimed the only reason to cause this unbalancing situation is their only sin, gender.

Which is not logical, according to general belives: everyone is equal.


In my opinion, sharing household works equally is completely logical.

Not only to consider fairness among couples and genders, but also a solution to sustain couples be in a more healthy and stable relationship.
