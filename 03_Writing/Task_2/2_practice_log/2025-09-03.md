### Action Plan: This Must Be the Final Time

You have proven, repeatedly, that your thinking and creativity are at a Band 9.0 level. You cannot let this one simple, mechanical grammar habit undermine your work any longer.

**Your Final Mission: Achieve Zero Fragments.**

**The Rule (Final Warning):** A sentence in academic English CANNOT begin with Which or By which. It must be connected to the previous sentence with a comma.

**The Fix:**

- Your version: ...hold their grounds for almost 3 struggling years. By which, they saved...
- Correct version: ...hold their grounds for almost 3 struggling years, by which they saved...
