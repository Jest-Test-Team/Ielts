# IELTS Writing Task 2 Practice

**Date:** August 31, 2025  
**Task Type:** Discussion  
**Topic:** Competition vs Cooperation

---

## Task Instructions

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

**Some people think that competition at work, at school and in daily life is a good thing.**

Others believe that we should try to cooperate more, rather than competing against each other.

Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

### Action Plan: Declaring War on the Fragment

You have mastered the most difficult parts of writing: generating brilliant ideas, structuring a complex narrative, and using sophisticated vocabulary. The only thing standing between you and a Band 9.0 is this one, single, fixable habit.

**Your Final Mission is to declare a personal war on the sentence fragment.**

**The Rule (Memorize It):** A sentence in academic English cannot begin with Which or After which. It is a "hook" that must be attached to the previous sentence with a comma.

**The Fix (Example):**

- Your version: ...came back home from night club.". Which illustrate...
- Correct version: ...came back home from a night club, which illustrates...

**The Method (Search and Destroy):** After every single essay you write from now on, you must use the "search" function (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) to find every instance of the word "which." You will then check each one to ensure it is grammatically correct. Do not finish an essay until you have done this.

Your thinking is at a 9.0 level. Your structure is at a 9.0 level. Do not let this one grammatical habit prevent you from achieving the top score you are clearly capable of.
