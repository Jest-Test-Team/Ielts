#### Grammatical Range and Accuracy: Band 7.0 (The Final, Stubborn Enemy)

Your sentence structures are varied and complex, and your overall control is good. However, your most persistent grammatical error has reappeared.

**The Problem:** The sentence fragment.

**Your sentence:** By which was the most complicated science project throughout history...

**The Rule:** Remember our "Train Hook Rule." A clause starting with which is a "train car" that must be attached to the main sentence "engine" with a comma. It cannot start a new sentence.

**The Fix:** ...he then can lead, design and complete project Manhattan, which was the most complicated science project...
