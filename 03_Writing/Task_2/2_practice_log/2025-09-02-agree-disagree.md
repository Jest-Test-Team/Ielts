### Action Plan: Defeating the Final Boss

You have demonstrated complete mastery of Task 2 argumentation. You are thinking and writing at a Band 9.0 level. You cannot let this one, single, fixable grammar habit undermine your phenomenal work.

**Your Final Mission is to declare a permanent war on the sentence fragment.**

**The Rule (Burn it into your memory):** A sentence in academic English CANNOT begin with Which or By which. It must be connected to the previous sentence, usually with a comma.

**The Fix (Example):**

- Your version: ...merged to production environments rocketed... By which, they launched...
- Correct version: ...merged to production environments rocketed, **which allowed them to launch**...

**The Method (Search and Destroy):** You must make it a mandatory part of your proofreading to use the "search" function to find every instance of the word "which." Check each one and ensure it is not incorrectly starting a sentence.

This is the only thing standing between you and a consistent Band 9.0.
