**Date:** August 31, 2025

**Topic:** Social Centre Activities in Melbourne

## Task Instructions

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

The graph below gives information on the numbers of participants for different activities at one social centre in Melbourne, Australia for the period 2000 to 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

---

## Feedback & Analysis

### Overall Assessment

This is another essay that showcases your excellent language control and structural skills. Your sentence discipline, grammar, and logical paragraphing are consistently at a very high level.

However, the final score is being held back from a 7.5 or higher by one critical data reporting error that falls into the "fatal flaw" category we have discussed.

**Overall Estimated Band Score: 7.0**

---

### Detailed Analysis

#### Cohesion & Grammar (CC & GRA): Band 7.5 - 8.0

**Strengths 👍:**

- **Perfect Structure:** Your chronological split (describing trends from 2000-2010 and then 2010-2020) is the ideal structure for this chart. Your organization is flawless.
- **Excellent Sentence Control:** Your sentences are clear, concise, and grammatically accurate. You have completely mastered the avoidance of sentence fragments. Your language foundation is a major strength.

#### Task Achievement (TA): Band 5.5 (The Fatal Flaw)

**Strengths 👍:** Your overview is excellent. You correctly identified the different overall trends for all the activities (stable, rising, and falling). Your reporting of most of the data points and trends is also very accurate.

**The Critical Error ✍️:**

- **The Problem:** In your second body paragraph, you described the number for table tennis as around 55%.
- **The Chart's Truth:** The Y-axis of the chart clearly shows the unit is the Number of participants, not a percentage.
- **The Impact:** This is the exact type of "Y-axis misreading" we have identified as a 'fatal flaw.' It shows a lack of attention to the details of the chart and is a major error in Task Achievement, even if the rest of your analysis is perfect. It unfortunately overshadows the high quality of your other observations.

#### Lexical Resource (Vocabulary): Band 6.0

**Strengths 👍:** You used some good descriptive vocabulary like fluctuated, rocketed, and declined.

**Areas for Improvement ✍️:**

- **Spelling:** material arts should be martial arts.
- **Phrasing:** The phrase the number of participating amateur dramatics is a bit awkward. A more natural phrasing is the number of participants **in** amateur dramatics.

---

### Action Plan: The Final Layer of Vigilance

- **Make the "Chart ID Card" Unbreakable:** Before you write a single word, you must force yourself to check the Y-axis and state the unit out loud (e.g., "Number of people," "Percentage," "Million tonnes"). This habit will prevent 99% of these fatal flaws.

- **Activate Your "Final Check" for Keywords:** Your "30-Second Final Check" should also include a quick scan for the spelling of keywords from the chart's legend (like martial arts).
