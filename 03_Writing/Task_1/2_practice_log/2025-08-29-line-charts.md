# IELTS Writing Task 1 Practice

**Date:** August 29, 2025  
**Task Type:** Line Chart  
**Topic:** Urban Population in Asian Countries

---

## Task Instructions

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

The graph below gives information about the percentage of the population in four Asian countries living in cities from 1970 to 2020, with predictions for 2030 and 2040.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

---

## Feedback & Analysis

### Overall Assessment

Let's analyze this essay. The good news is that your language control—your grammar and sentence structure—is excellent. The structure you chose for the essay is also perfect.

However, the bad news is that this essay contains some of the most critical data reporting errors we have seen. You have unfortunately misread the chart's legend and confused the data for two of the countries, which severely impacts your Task Achievement score.

**Overall Estimated Band Score: 6.0**

---

### Detailed Analysis

#### Grammar & Cohesion (GRA & CC): Band 7.0 - 7.5

**Strengths 👍:**

- **Excellent Sentence Control:** Your sentences are clear, concise, and you have completely eliminated sentence fragments. Your grammatical accuracy is a major strength.
- **Perfect Structure:** Your decision to split the essay chronologically (trends before 2020 and predictions after 2020) is the ideal structure for this line graph. Your organization is flawless.

#### Task Achievement (TA): Band 4.0 (The Fatal Flaw)

This is where the essay faces major problems that severely limit your overall score.

**1. Incorrect Overview:**
Your overview states that the Philippines' urban population decreased. While it did dip after 1990, the overall trend from start (1970) to finish (2040) is a clear increase (from ~31% to over 50%). The overview must report the main trend over the entire period.

**2. Confusing the Countries (The Most Critical Error):**
In your first body paragraph, you have completely confused the data for the Philippines and Indonesia.

- **Your description:** You wrote that Indonesia experienced a decline from 1990 to 2010.
- **The Chart's Truth:** The country that peaked around 1990 and then declined was the Philippines. Indonesia, in contrast, started as the lowest and showed a steady, uninterrupted increase throughout the entire period.
- **The Impact:** This is a fundamental error in reporting the data. It shows a misreading of the chart's legend, which is the most critical part of the task.

---

### Action Plan: Back to the Battle Plan

This essay is a perfect example of how a Band 8.0 in language can be brought down to a 6.0 by a Band 4.0 in data accuracy. The lesson is simple but crucial.

You must return to and strictly enforce the planning and checking stages of your "作戰計畫" (Battle Plan).

- **The "Chart ID Card" is Non-Negotiable:** Before you write a single word, you must identify each line. Point to the dotted line and say "Malaysia." Point to the line with squares and say "Philippines." Do this for all four countries.

- **Upgrade Your "Read Aloud Preview":** After you identify each line, trace it with your finger and say its main trend out loud.
  - Example: Point to the line with squares. "Okay, Philippines: starts at 30, up to 50 in 1990, then down, then up again." Point to the line with triangles. "Okay, Indonesia: starts lowest, steady increase, overtakes the others."
  - This two-minute process will 100% prevent you from confusing the data and will ensure your overview is accurate.

You have mastered the most difficult parts of writing: grammar, structure, and sentence control. Your language skills are ready for a top score. Now, you must ensure you are applying those excellent skills to the correct information.
