# IELTS Writing Task 1: Destinations of History Graduates - Mixed Charts 📊

📋 Task Information

Question Type	Topic	Word Count	Estimated Score
Mixed Charts (Pie + Table)	Destinations of UK History Graduates	150+ words	Band 6.0

🎯 Question Prompt

    The chart below shows what History graduates did after completing their undergraduate degree course from a university in the UK. The table shows the sectors these graduates were employed in within six years of graduating and their average incomes.

    Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

[Analysis is based on the prior evaluation of your essay.]

🔍 Detailed Analysis

Task Achievement: Band 4.5 🎯

Strengths:

    ✅ High-Level Analysis Potential: Demonstrated the ability for brilliant analysis with a mathematically accurate comparison of the 'Other' and 'Archaeologist' sectors ("marginally more than six times").

Areas for Improvement:

    ❌ Fundamental Misinterpretation: The essay incorrectly identified the subjects as "postgraduate students" instead of undergraduate graduates.

    ❌ Major Data Errors: There were critical errors in reporting salary figures (e.g., writing £3,000 instead of £30,000, and £320,000 instead of £32,000).

    ❌ Inaccurate Comparison: The mathematical comparison between the teacher (30%) and solicitor (18%) percentages was incorrect.

Coherence and Cohesion: Band 6.5 🔗

Strengths:

    ✅ Logical Structure: The four-paragraph structure (Intro, Overview, Pie Chart, Table) was perfect for this task.

Areas for Improvement:

    ❌ Messy Paragraphing: The second body paragraph was structurally weak, ending with several summary sentences that belonged in the overview.

Lexical Resource (Vocabulary): Band 6.5 💬

Strengths:

    ✅ Sufficient Range: The vocabulary was generally strong and sufficient to discuss the data clearly.

Areas for Improvement:

    ❌ Overshadowed by TA Errors: The overall quality of the vocabulary was undermined by the major factual errors in the essay.

Grammatical Range and Accuracy: Band 6.5 📚

Strengths:

    ✅ Excellent Sentence Control: For the most part, sentences were clear, concise, and you successfully eliminated sentence fragments.

Areas for Improvement:

    ❌ Loss of Discipline: One "monster sentence" appeared, which disrupted the otherwise excellent sentence control.

🚀 Action Plan for Improvement

Immediate Actions:

    Slow Down & Read Meticulously: Dedicate a full 60 seconds at the start to read every single word of the prompt, chart titles, and labels.

    Execute a Ruthless Final Check: Use the "30-Second Final Check" to verify every number and key fact you have written against the source material.

Long-term Goals:

    Internalize the "Battle Plan": Make the pre-writing (Step 1) and post-writing (Step 7) checks an unbreakable habit.

    Maintain Discipline Under Pressure: Ensure that even when analyzing complex data, your established skills (sentence control, paragraph structure) are maintained from start to finish.

💡 Key Learning Points

    Data Accuracy is the Foundation: Excellent language skills cannot save an essay if the foundational data is wrong. Task Achievement is the base upon which a high score is built.

    The First and Last Minutes are Critical: The initial, careful reading of the prompt and the final, meticulous proofread are the most important moments to prevent "fatal flaws."
