## Detailed Analysis

Task Achievement: Band 7.0

    Strengths 👍: Your overview is excellent. You correctly identified the main feature: the inverse relationship between population percentage and wealth distribution. You have also accurately reported most of the key figures for the different regions.

Coherence and Cohesion: Band 5.5 (The Main Weakness)

    The Problem: The most significant issue in this essay is the lack of paragraphing. You have written the entire response—from the overview to the detailed descriptions—as one single, long paragraph.

    Why This Is a Problem: In IELTS, clear paragraphing is essential for a high score. An essay must be organized into logical paragraphs (Introduction, Overview, Body 1, Body 2) to be easy to read and understand. A single block of text is considered a major structural flaw.

A Better Structure:

    Introduction: The chart demonstrates...

    Overview: In general, regions with...

    Body Paragraph 1 (Wealthy Regions): North America, with just below...

    Body Paragraph 2 (Less Wealthy Regions): On the contrary, the percentages of global wealth...

Lexical Resource & Grammar (LR & GRA): Band 6.5

    Strengths 👍: Your sentence control is good, and you make some effective comparisons. Your vocabulary is sufficient for the task.

    Areas for Improvement ✍️:

        Spelling & Typos: populaution (should be population).

        Awkward Phrasing: Following with Europe should be Followed by Europe. the percentage of of global wealth (repeated "of").

        Superlative Form: largestest should simply be largest.

## Action Plan

You have the analytical skills to write a high-scoring essay, but you must present your ideas in the correct format.

    Master the Four-Paragraph Structure: This is non-negotiable for Task 1. Always separate your Introduction, Overview, first Body Paragraph, and second Body Paragraph.

    Continue Proofreading: Keep checking for the small spelling and grammar errors.

By simply adding the correct paragraph breaks, this essay's score would immediately jump to a 7.0 or higher.
