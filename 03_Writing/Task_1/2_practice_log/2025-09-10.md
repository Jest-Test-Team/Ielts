# IELTS Writing Task 1 - Process Diagram

**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Word Count:** At least 150 words

## Question

The diagram below shows how rain water is collected and then treated to be used as drinking water in an Australian town. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

---

## Answer

The diagram depicts the process of collecting rain water as well as transforming it into drinking water in a town located in Australian. 


In general, there are several phases included within this process pipeline, containing collecting,filtering, storing, processing

,delivring, and transporting.


First, rainwater fell on roof will be gathered into underground drain through pipes.

Then all the rainwater gathered from different locations will enter water filter.

The next step for these post-filtered water will be contained in underground storage space.


After that, water treatment with chemicals involved will be implemented in another underground space using water from last step transmitted through pipe.

Lastly, the final step will be bringing the processed water back to houses on ground via pipe and provide drinking water.

Thus complete the whole process cycle.
