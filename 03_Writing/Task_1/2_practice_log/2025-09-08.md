# IELTS Writing Task 1 - Bar Chart

**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Word Count:** At least 150 words

## Question

The chart shows British Emigration to selected destinations between 2004 and 2007.

Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

---

## Answer

The chart gives information about 5 destination countries of  British immigrants from 2004 to 2007.


Overall, the numbers of immigrants selected Spain and New Zealand declined steadily in general speaking, while the numbers of other countries fluctuated.


Immigrants number selecting Australia, started with just over 40 thousands in 2004, had dropped slightly to just below 40 thousand in 2005.

While the number of choosing the USA declined from more than 20 thousand to less than 20 thousand in the same period.

However, number for France inclined sharply, from just above 20 thousand in 2004, to more than 30 thousand in 2005.

As for Spain, the number marginally increased, nearly not being able to be observed, was around 35 thousand in both these 2 years.

And  the number slightly declined for New Zealand , which was also hard to be observed, both just above 20 thousand in these two years.


When it came to 2006, immigrants number selected Australia, increased with a noteworthy amount, more than 10 thousand, reached more than 50 thousand, being highest number in all countries and all time in the graph.

But declined to just above 40 thousand in 2007.

The number of the USA in 2006 was close to that of 2004, and declined to exact 20 thousand in 2007.

And  France encountered large decline during this time period, with around 25 thousand in 2006, and less than 20 thousand in 2007, which was the lowest number among all countries and all time in the graph.

Lastly, the number for Spain and New Zealand both declined from 2006 to 2007, while the former dropped from above 30 thousand to below, and New Zealand dropped from more than 20 thousand to exact 20 thousand.

## Learning Feedback

This is a good attempt at a complex bar chart, and your structure is very clear. You have successfully identified many of the key figures.

However, your Task Achievement is seriously undermined by misreading a key trend, and your Grammar score is limited by the return of some old habits.

**Overall Estimated Band Score: 6.5**

## Detailed Analysis

Task Achievement: Band 6.0 (The Main Weakness)

    Strengths 👍: You accurately reported most of the individual data points for each year. Your comparison of Australia in 2006 being the highest peak and France in 2007 being the lowest point is a good selection of key features.

    The Critical Interpretation Error ✍️:

        Your Overview: You stated that the number of emigrants to Spain "declined steadily."

        The Chart's Truth: Spain's numbers are approximately 35k (2004), 34k (2005), 32k (2006), and 29k (2007). This is a consistent and clear downward trend. Your description here is correct.

        However, your overview also says the numbers for "other countries fluctuated." While there are fluctuations, the most significant trend you missed is that emigration to Australia showed a clear overall increase from start to finish (from ~42k to ~45k, despite the peak and dip). The most important features are the overall trends, not just the year-to-year movements. A better overview would have been: "Overall, emigration to Australia increased, while the numbers for Spain, New Zealand, and the USA saw a general decline. France was the most volatile, rising and then falling sharply."

Grammar, Cohesion, and Vocabulary (GRA, LR & CC): Band 6.5 - 7.0

    Strengths 👍:

        Good Structure (CC): Your chronological split (2004-2005 vs. 2006-2007) is a logical way to organize the data.

        Good Sentence Control (GRA): For the most part, you have maintained the "short sentence" discipline, which keeps your writing clear.

    The Recurring Enemies ✍️:

        The "Monster Sentence": One long, uncontrolled sentence has reappeared in your first body paragraph:

            And the number slightly declined for New Zealand , which was also hard to be observed, both just above 20 thousand in these two years. This sentence is awkward and grammatically incorrect.

        Vocabulary Precision (LR):

            Immigrants number selecting Australia: A more natural phrasing is "The number of emigrants choosing Australia."

            declined from more than 20 thousand to below: To below what? This is imprecise. Be specific: "...to below 30 thousand."

            inclined sharply: The correct verb for an upward trend is increased or rose.

## Action Plan: Master the Overview

You have proven you can control your language and structure. The next and most important step is to perfect your analysis in the planning stage.

    Look for the "Big Story": Before you start writing, look at the start point (2004) and the end point (2007) for each country. This will tell you the overall trend. Is the number higher or lower at the end? This should be the main feature in your overview.

    Describe the Journey: Use the body paragraphs to describe the fluctuations between the start and end points (the peaks and troughs).

Your language skills are strong. By focusing on identifying the most significant overall trends for your overview, you will ensure your excellent language is used to describe the most important information.
